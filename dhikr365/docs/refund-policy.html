Adhkar 365 — Last updated: September 27, 2026

Honest, Transparent & Fair: At Adhkar 365, all core Islamic features (duas, authentic audio, prayer times, Qibla compass) are 100% free and ad-free. For voluntary supporter packages and Pro upgrades, we believe in complete financial transparency with zero hidden fees and no deceptive traps.

1. Google Play In-App Purchases & Refunds

All digital transactions in the Android version of Adhkar 365 are processed through Google Play Billing. Google Play governs the billing and refund processes according to its consumer protection standards.

1.1 Requesting a Refund via Google Play (Within 48 Hours)

If you made an accidental purchase or are unsatisfied with your Pro upgrade, you can request a full refund directly from Google within 48 hours of purchase:

  1. Visit the Google Play Order History page: Google Play Order History.
  2. Find the order you want to return.
  3. Select Request a refund or Report a problem and choose the option that describes your situation.
  4. Complete the form and submit. Google typically issues refund decisions within 1 business day (often within minutes).

1.2 Requesting Developer Assistance (After 48 Hours)

If more than 48 hours have passed or if you experience technical issues restoring your purchase, we are here to help. Contact us at abdullahalmasum365@gmail.com with:

  • Your Google Play Order Transaction ID (format: GPA.XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXX, found on your email receipt from Google Play).
  • The email address linked to your Google Play account.
  • A brief description of the issue.

2. Managing and Canceling Subscriptions

If you choose a recurring monthly or annual voluntary supporter package:

  • Cancel Anytime: You can cancel your subscription at any time via the Google Play app: Google Play Store → Profile Icon → Payments & Subscriptions → Subscriptions → Adhkar 365 → Cancel Subscription.
  • Access Until End of Billing Cycle: When you cancel, you will continue to have access to Pro features until the end of the current paid billing period without any cancellation fees.
  • No Auto-Trap or Hidden Fees: Pricing is clearly displayed in your local currency prior to confirmation. There are no setup fees, hidden maintenance charges, or termination penalties.
