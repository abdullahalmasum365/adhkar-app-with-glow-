Refund & Subscription Policy

Adhkar 365 — Last updated: October 20, 2026

Honest, Transparent & Fair: Adhkar 365 is completely free and 100% ad-free for everyone. All core Islamic features (all supplications, authentic Arabic audio, local prayer times, color themes, custom routines, and Qibla compass) are freely accessible to all users worldwide without paywalls. For users who choose optional voluntary maintenance support, we believe in complete financial transparency, simple cancellation, and no hidden traps.

1. Google Play In-App Billing & Refunds

All digital transactions in the Android version of Adhkar 365 are processed securely through Google Play Billing. Google Play administers the billing, tax collection, and refund processes in accordance with its consumer protection policies.

1.1 Requesting a Direct Refund via Google Play (Within 48 Hours)

If you made an accidental support contribution or subscription, you can request an instant refund directly from Google within 48 hours of purchase:

  1. Open the Google Play Store order history: Google Play Order History.
  2. Locate the Adhkar 365 order you wish to refund.
  3. Select Request a refund or Report a problem and select the relevant reason.
  4. Submit the request. Google typically issues refund decisions within 1 business day (and often within minutes).

1.2 Requesting Developer Assistance (After 48 Hours)

If more than 48 hours have elapsed or if you experience technical issues, contact our support team at abdullahalmasum365@gmail.com with:

  • Your Google Play Order Transaction ID (format: GPA.XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXX from your Google Play email receipt).
  • The Google account email address used for the purchase.
  • A brief description of your request.

2. Voluntary Maintenance Subscription Tiers & Pricing

Users may choose to support ongoing engineering, server infrastructure, and app maintenance through three flexible subscription options:

  • 1 Month Maintenance Support: $5 / month (renews monthly).
  • 6 Months Sustainer (Semi-Annual): $30 / 6 months (renews every 6 months).
  • 12 Months Sponsor (Annual): $60 / year (renews annually).

Note: Exact prices are determined by Google Play in your local currency and include applicable local taxes. There are no signup fees, unexpected surcharges, or termination fees.

3. Managing and Canceling Subscriptions

You have complete control over your subscription at all times:

  • Cancel Anytime: You can cancel your subscription at any time directly through Google Play: Google Play Store app → Profile Icon → Payments & Subscriptions → Subscriptions → Adhkar 365 → Cancel Subscription.
  • Active Until Expiration: When you cancel, you will continue to enjoy your supporter status and Muhsin badge until the end of your current paid billing period. No further renewal charges will occur.
  • No Locked Devotional Features: Because all core devotional features (adhkar, du'as, audio, prayer times, Qibla) are 100% free and open, canceling a subscription will never disable, lock, or degrade any Islamic remembrance tools.

4. Digital Supporter Recognition & Legal Classification

In recognition of voluntary maintenance contributions:

  • Digital Recognition Badge: Supporters receive a digital "Muhsin" (মুহসিন) patron badge on their profile screen for the duration of active support.
  • Du'a Dedication Certificate: Supporters have the option to generate, personalize, and download a digital Du'a Dedication Certificate in honor of parents, family, deceased loved ones, or themselves.
  • Developer Patronage (Not a Charitable Donation): All support contributions are digital software in-app purchases processed through Google Play Billing. They are NOT tax-deductible charitable contributions to a registered 501(c)(3) or tax-exempt charitable trust, and no charitable tax receipts are issued. All funds go directly to the independent developer (Abdullah Al Masum) to cover application maintenance, API costs, bug fixes, and keeping the service 100% free and ad-free worldwide.
