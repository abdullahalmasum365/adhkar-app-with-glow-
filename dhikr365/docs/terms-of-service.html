1. Purpose of the Application

Adhkar 365 is a non-commercial, spiritual utility designed to assist Muslims worldwide in the daily remembrance of Allah (dhikr), reciting authentic supplications (du'as) from Hisnul Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim), calculating local prayer times, and determining the Qibla direction. The core app functions fully offline.
