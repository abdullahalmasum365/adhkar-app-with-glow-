1. Purpose of the Application & Operating Model

Adhkar 365 is an independent, 100% ad-free Islamic spiritual utility designed to assist Muslims worldwide in the daily remembrance of Allah (dhikr), reciting authentic supplications (du'as) from Hisnul Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim), calculating local prayer times, and determining the Qibla direction. The core Islamic features (all daily adhkar categories, verified Arabic audio recitations, local prayer calculations, and Qibla compass) are completely free for all users forever.
