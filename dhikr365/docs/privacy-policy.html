Privacy Policy

Adhkaar 365 — Last updated: July 31, 2026

This policy explains what information Adhkaar 365 ("the app") collects, why, and how it is used. The app is built to work fully offline for anyone who never signs in — an account is entirely optional and only adds cross-device sync of your favorites and custom plan.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Location

With your permission, the app uses your device's GPS location (or a city you search and select manually) to calculate accurate prayer times and Islamic calendar dates for your area. Location is stored only on your device unless you sign in (see 1.3 below), and is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.

1.2 Notifications

The app schedules local reminders (prayer times, morning/evening adhkar) directly on your device using your chosen settings. No notification content or schedule is sent to any server.

1.3 Account Information (only if you choose to sign in)

Signing in with Google or Apple is entirely optional. If you choose to sign in, we receive and store:

  • Your name and email address, as provided by Google/Apple
  • A unique account identifier
  • Your favorite duas and custom dhikr plan, so they can be restored on any device you sign in on

This data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising. You can delete your account data at any time by contacting us (see Section 5).

1.4 Information We Do Not Collect

  • No advertising identifiers or ad tracking
  • No third-party analytics or behavioral tracking
  • No payment card details are ever seen or stored by the app — donations/subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play Billing under Google's own privacy policy

2. How We Use Information

  • To calculate accurate prayer times for your location
  • To schedule and deliver the reminders you enable
  • To restore your favorites/custom plan when you sign in on a new device

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone. Data is only shared with the service providers that make the app function:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) — for optional account sign-in and sync
  • Google Play Billing — for processing optional donations/subscriptions

4. Your Choices

  • Location permission can be denied or revoked at any time in your device settings — the app still works, using manually entered location instead
  • Notification permission can be turned off at any time
  • You can sign out of your account at any time from Settings → Account & Sync
  • You may request full deletion of your account data at any time (see Section 5)

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
