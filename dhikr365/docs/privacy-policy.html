Privacy Policy

Adhkar 365 — Last updated: September 27, 2026

Privacy First Commitment: Adhkar 365 is designed with privacy at its core. The app is 100% ad-free, does not track your personal activities, does not profile users, and does not use advertising identifiers. The core Islamic features (daily adhkar, audio playback, prayer calculations, and offline city database) work fully offline without an internet connection. Network access is used strictly for optional features: cloud sync, small-town search fallback via OpenStreetMap Nominatim, runtime Google Fonts typography delivery, and Google Play Billing.

This Privacy Policy describes how Adhkar 365 ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed and maintained by Abdullah Al Masum, collects, protects, and handles information.

1. Information We Collect and Why (Data Minimization)

We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization. We only access device resources that are strictly necessary to provide Islamic remembrance, prayer schedules, and optional account synchronization.

1.1 Location Data (Coarse & Precise)

  • Purpose: Used exclusively to calculate local Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and the Qibla compass direction using astronomical calculations (Adhan algorithm).
  • Local Processing: All mathematical prayer calculations occur locally on your device. Your GPS coordinates are never uploaded to our servers, never stored remotely, and never sold or shared with any commercial parties.
  • Offline World City Database: The app bundles a pre-compiled offline database of over 34,000 cities worldwide (GeoNames cities15000, licensed CC-BY 4.0). You can select your city manually without granting GPS permission or requiring an internet connection.
  • Online Small Town Search Fallback (OpenStreetMap Nominatim): If you search for an uncommon small town not found in the bundled offline database, the app makes an HTTPS geocoding request to nominatim.openstreetmap.org (operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation) to resolve the town name into geographic coordinates. In accordance with Nominatim's strict Usage Policy, requests transmit our verified contact User-Agent: Adhkaar365App/1.0 (https://github.com/abdullahalmasum365/adhkar-app-with-glow-; abdullahalmasum365@gmail.com). Only the user-typed search string is transmitted; your real-time GPS coordinates, user account, and device identifiers are NEVER transmitted to Nominatim. The OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy applies to these geocoding queries.

1.2 Local Notifications & Exact Alarms

  • Purpose: To trigger time-sensitive alerts for Morning & Evening Adhkar and prayer calls at their prescribed astronomical times.
  • Handling: Notification schedules are handled locally using your device's native alarm scheduler (AlarmManager on Android). No schedule or notification data is sent to external servers.

1.3 Optional Account Information (Only If You Choose to Sign In)

Signing in with Google or Apple is 100% optional. If you choose to sign in to sync your custom dhikr routine across devices, we store:

  • Your display name and email address provided by your authentication provider (Google or Apple).
  • A secure, pseudonymous user identifier (UID).
  • Your custom routine list and favorite dhikr IDs, to restore them upon signing in on another device.

1.4 Purchase History & Supporter Sponsorships

  • Processing: All optional Pro upgrades and voluntary supporter sponsorships are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing.
  • Data Handled: We receive only anonymized purchase tokens and product IDs to activate features and verify active subscription states across devices.
  • No Card Data: We NEVER receive, process, or store your credit card, debit card, or banking details.

1.5 Offline Pre-Bundled Typography & Google Fonts Engine

  • Pre-Bundled Font Assets: All primary font families utilized across the app (including Amiri Arabic, Manrope, Noto Serif diacritic transliteration, Space Mono, and Public Sans) are pre-bundled directly within the application's local asset bundle. These open-source fonts render 100% offline without initiating network requests or transmitting device IP addresses to font CDNs during standard usage.
  • Fallback Runtime Fetching: The application incorporates the open-source google_fonts Flutter library. In the rare event that an unbundled font style is requested, the library connects over HTTPS to Google Fonts CDN (fonts.gstatic.com). In such cases, Google LLC processes technical device IP and standard HTTP request headers solely to deliver the font binary. No personal data, user accounts, or analytics are collected or profiled.

1.6 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Advertising IDs: We do NOT collect Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Apple IDFA.
  • No Cookies or Tracking Pixels: We do not use web cookies, flash cookies, or tracking beacons in the mobile application. (See our Cookie Policy).
  • No Biometric or Contact Data: We do not access your contacts, phone state, microphone, camera, or photo gallery.

5. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In full compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, you have the right to permanently erase your account and all associated cloud data at any time:

Option A: Instant In-App Deletion

  1. Open the Adhkar 365 app.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & Sync.
  3. Tap "Delete Account" at the bottom of the card.
  4. Confirm the prompt. Your profile, authentication credentials, and synced custom routines are instantly and permanently purged from Google Firebase servers.

Option B: Web / Email Deletion Request (Without Reinstalling the App)

If you uninstalled the App or cannot access your device, submit a data deletion request by emailing:

Email: abdullahalmasum365@gmail.com

Subject: Adhkar 365 Account Deletion Request

Include the email address associated with your account. We will permanently delete all your data within 7 business days and confirm by email.
