Privacy Policy

Adhkaar 365 — Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Adhkaar 365 ("the App", "we", "us") collects, why, and how it is protected. The App is built from the ground up to respect your privacy and works fully offline for anyone who never signs in. An account is entirely optional and only enables cross-device synchronization of your favorites and custom dhikr routines.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Location Data

With your explicit permission, the App uses your device's GPS coordinates (or a city you manually select) solely to compute accurate Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and Qibla direction. Location data is processed locally on your device, is never transmitted to any external server, and is never shared or used for advertising.

1.2 Notifications & Alarms

The App schedules prayer alerts and morning/evening adhkar reminders locally on your device using your selected preferences. No notification schedule or personal timing is sent to any server.

1.3 Account Information (Only If You Choose to Sign In)

Signing in with Google or Apple is 100% optional. If you choose to sign in, we securely store:

  • Your name and email address (provided by Google or Apple).
  • A unique authentication user identifier (UID).
  • Your enabled dhikr preferences and custom routine list, so they are restored when you log in on another device.

This data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). It is never sold, shared, or used for commercial marketing.

1.4 Information We Do Not Collect

  • No Web Cookies or Tracking Pixels: Adhkaar 365 is a native mobile application. We do not use web cookies, flash cookies, or tracking beacons.
  • No Advertising Identifiers or Ad Trackers: We do not collect Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Apple IDFA, and we do not serve third-party ads.
  • No Invasive Analytics: We do not track your clicks, reading habits, or behavioral data.
  • No Payment Card Information: All voluntary donations and Pro upgrades are handled exclusively by Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple In-App Purchase (iOS). We never receive, process, or store your credit/debit card numbers.

2. How We Use Information

  • To calculate precise prayer times for your local coordinates
  • To deliver prayer and dhikr reminders you have activated
  • To restore your saved custom dhikr routine when you switch phones or sign in on a new device

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is processed exclusively by essential infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore) — for optional account login and cloud backup.
  • Google Play Billing — for handling optional donations and Pro upgrades securely under Google's policies.

4. Your Choices & Permissions

  • Location Permission: Can be denied or revoked anytime in system settings. The App will seamlessly function using manual city selection.
  • Notification Permission: Can be turned on or off at any time in Settings.
  • Sign Out: You can sign out at any time from Settings → Account & Sync.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's User Data and Account Deletion policies, you have complete control over your account. You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud records at any time:

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the Adhkaar 365 app.
  2. Navigate to Settings → Account & Sync.
  3. Tap "Delete Account" at the bottom of the card.
  4. Confirm the prompt. Your profile and all synced custom routines will be instantly and permanently deleted from Google Firebase.

Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request (Without reinstalling the App)

If you have uninstalled the App or cannot access it, you can request full account and data deletion by sending an email from your registered address:

Email: abdullahalmasum365@gmail.com

Subject: Adhkaar 365 Account Deletion Request

Your request will be processed, and all associated Firebase records will be permanently wiped within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.
