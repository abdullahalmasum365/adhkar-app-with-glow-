Cookie Policy

Adhkar 365 — Last updated: September 27, 2026

No Cookies Used: Adhkar 365 is a native mobile application. We do not use web cookies, flash cookies, tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking beacons.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser or device by websites to store preferences, session information, or track user activity across web domains.

2. How We Handle Cookies

2.1 Native Mobile Application

The Adhkar 365 mobile app runs natively on Android and iOS devices. It stores user preferences (such as selected language, prayer calculation method, and dhikr counts) using your device's native local key-value store (SharedPreferences on Android, UserDefaults on iOS). These are local storage mechanisms essential to app functioning and are not web cookies. They never track you across other applications.

2.2 Static Documentation Website

Our policy documentation pages (hosted via GitHub Pages) are purely static HTML documents designed to inform users of their privacy rights. We do not set tracking cookies, commercial analytics scripts, or advertising beacons on this documentation site.

3. Cookie Consent Banner Status

Under the EU ePrivacy Directive (Cookie Law) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), a cookie consent banner is required only when non-essential tracking, advertising, or profiling cookies are deployed. Because Adhkar 365 does not deploy any tracking or advertising cookies whatsoever, no cookie consent banner is needed or displayed.
